Injuries test (API-Football, 1 request)

Asks who is unavailable for one club. Team id 541 = Real Madrid, 85 = PSG. Leave season and date empty to get the club's current list. The answer appears in "Raw answer".

Catch up older matches (API-Football, 1 request per day)

Looks up one past day at a time, newest first (10 days per press), and queues full stats for every older finished match of that day in the five big leagues and the Champions League. The stats themselves are then fetched by the normal job, 1 request each. If the plan refuses past days, the reason shows here and nothing more is spent.

Matches without full stats

Downloads a CSV of every finished match this season that has no full stats yet, with its status (waiting for stats, or no fixture id).

Put recovered match stats into the app (CSV)

Choose the recovered CSV file. Shots, corners, fouls and cards are saved on each match whose score agrees with ours. Matches that already have fuller stats are kept as they are. Safe to repeat.

Fetch season top scorers now (football-data.org)

Saves the full-season scorers list that the app shows on the Stats tab. One request per league, about 6 seconds apart, so "All leagues" takes about 35 seconds. The server also refreshes them by itself every 6 hours.

Update birth dates and nationality (football-data.org)

Reads every club's squad (one request per league, about 6 seconds apart, so "All leagues" takes about 30 seconds) and compares it with players we already have. "Overwrite" makes football-data.org win when it disagrees (the old values are saved first so this can be undone) and lists every conflict. "Preview" changes nothing and only lists the conflicts. "Fill gaps only" never touches an existing value. No new players are added. Safe to repeat.

Put Bundesliga goals into the app (OpenLigaDB)

Reads every played Bundesliga matchday and saves the goals (scorer, minute, penalty, own goal) on each match whose goals add up to our score. Stats already saved are kept. Safe to repeat.

Older matches from Highlightly (Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Ligue 1)

1) Load the lists once (about 20 requests). 2) Fetch: 15 matches per press, 2 requests each, with goals, assists, cards, substitutions and line-ups. The server also fetches 2 matches every 20 minutes by itself, up to 45 matches a day, so the old ones fill in over about a week. Highlightly has no ratings.
