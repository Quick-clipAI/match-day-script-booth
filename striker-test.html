Catch up older matches (API-Football, 1 request per day)

Looks up one past day at a time, newest first (10 days per press), and queues full stats for every older finished match of that day in the five big leagues and the Champions League. The stats themselves are then fetched by the normal job, 1 request each. If the plan refuses past days, the reason shows here and nothing more is spent.
