Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026
A short, plain-language summary for an early-stage version of Creator Booth. It will be expanded as the product matures.

1. What we store

If you're signed inYour email address, the chats/messages you create, and any personalization info you choose to give us (see below), are stored in our Supabase database so you can return to them from any device.
If you're not signed in (guest)Your chats and any personalization info are stored only in your browser's local storage. We don't receive or store them.
Sign-in methodGoogle OAuth (we receive your name/email from Google) or email magic link — we don't see or store passwords either way.

2. Personalization information

Right after your first sign-in, and any time after in Settings → Profile, we may ask for: your surname, other names, a preferred nickname, how you heard about Creator Booth, what you plan to use it for, your favorite club, and any freeform notes you'd like to add — plus any script-writing preferences you set separately.

  • Every question except surname is optional, and you can skip the whole thing.
  • This information is used only to personalize your experience — for example, greeting you by name and, where relevant, tailoring scripts — and for nothing else. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to train models.
  • You can view, edit, or clear it at any time from Settings → Profile — changes take effect immediately.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Profile → Delete Account) permanently deletes this information along with your chats.

3. What we send to third parties

To generate a response, the text of your request is sent to one or more of the following, only as needed to answer it:

  • Google Gemini — to draft scripts and, for some requests, run a live Google Search for current facts.
  • Groq — as an automatic backup if Gemini is temporarily unavailable.
  • API-Football — to look up real match, club, and coaching data relevant to football-mode requests.

Personalization info (section 2) is not sent to these providers unless it's part of the message you're actively sending.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use your chats to train our own models.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use local storage (not cookies) to keep you signed in and, for guests, to store chats and personalization info on-device. There's no third-party ad tracking in Creator Booth.

5. Your controls

  • You can delete your account, all stored chats, and all personalization info at any time from Settings → Profile → Delete Account.
  • You can edit or clear your personalization info alone, without deleting your account, from Settings → Profile.
  • You can sign out at any time from Settings.
  • Guest data can be cleared by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

6. Data retention

Signed-in data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Guest data lives only in your browser and is never sent to us.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Creator Booth develops. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the app's Help & FAQ section.
